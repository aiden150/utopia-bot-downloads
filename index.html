A few things to know.

How do I install it?

On Mac, open the DMG and drag Utopia Bot into Applications. On Windows, open the EXE and follow the installer. Keep your existing app data to retain your collection.

Do the coffee promises charge me?

No. Clicking the promise unlocks a companion immediately. There is no payment form or charge.

What’s inside the fifth egg?

A unique companion with a few surprises of its own. Collect the first four to discover it.
